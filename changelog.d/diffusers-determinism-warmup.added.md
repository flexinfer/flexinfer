- `server-diffusers.py` gains two opt-in switches, both off by default. `TORCH_DETERMINISTIC=1` turns on `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(True, warn_only=True)` with uninitialized-memory filling off: on ROCm that is the only way to turn rocBLAS atomics off under PyTorch, and it also selects deterministic MIOpen solvers and Inductor's deterministic mode. `WARMUP_VIDEO_FRAMES` (with the lane's negative prompt) and `WARMUP_STEPS` let a text-to-video lane warm the exact clip shape and step count it serves.
