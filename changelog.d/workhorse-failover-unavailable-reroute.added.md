- Serve a request for an unavailable lane on its Ready equivalent in flexinfer-proxy. A Model annotated `flexinfer.ai/unavailable-reroute-to: <model>` that is Failed, parked behind a `primary/`, `gpu-lease/` or `gaming/` hold, or queued behind a higher-priority holder has the request served once on that Model when it is Ready with a closed breaker, under the target's lane budget; otherwise the request takes its normal path. Counted in `flexinfer_proxy_unavailable_reroute_total` (`internal/proxy/unavailable_reroute.go`, `internal/proxy/proxy.go`).
