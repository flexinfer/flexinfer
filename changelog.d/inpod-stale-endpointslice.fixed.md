Switching a live lane from its own Deployment to in-pod serving no longer leaves a dead backend behind its Service. Kubernetes stops managing a Service's EndpointSlices once the selector is removed, so the Deployment pod's address stayed `ready` after the pod was deleted and about half the connections to the ClusterIP failed (502 `lane_unresponsive` on the 7900 XTX warm lane, 2026-11-18). The controller now deletes the selector-built slices when it makes a Service selectorless, repeats that for two minutes, and heals Services an older controller switched. The controller ClusterRole gains `delete` and `deletecollection` on `endpointslices`.
