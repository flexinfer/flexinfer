- **vLLM plugins: prefill chunk sizing for mixed short and long requests (opt-in, off by default).** `FLEXINFER_PREFILL_SOLO_CAP=<tokens>` caps a request that is the only one eligible, which vLLM otherwise leaves uncapped, and `FLEXINFER_PREFILL_CAP_EXEMPT=<tokens>` exempts a request with at most that many tokens left from `--long-prefill-token-threshold` (plugin-common 0.8.0, text plugin 0.14.0). The plugin rewrites three lines of `Scheduler.schedule`, accepted by sha256 of vLLM 0.33.0+rocm72's scheduler.py; an unknown source fails the boot that set either variable. A step trace on the 5930k workhorse (2026-11-25) put a 2K request's 8-10 s time to first token beside a 56K prefill at 3.8-5.0 s behind one uncapped step plus 3.9-5.1 s of five capped steps of its own. `scripts/probe-prefill-step-trace.py` is that trace as a tool. No lane sets either variable; the engine canary is pre-registered as row `gfx1100-xtx-prefill-sched-2026-11-25` (pending).
