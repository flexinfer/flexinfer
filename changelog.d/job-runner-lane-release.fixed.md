A shared GPU now returns to the lane it was taken from when background jobs finish. A job's lane kept the card for its whole `serverless.idleTimeout` after the last job (17 min on the 7900 XTX in KT-JOBS, 2026-11-17). The proxy's job runner now writes `status.releasedAt` on the lane when no running job and no next queued job for that card uses it, and the controller treats a release that no request has answered for 45 s as idle: the lane scales to `minReplicas` and stops holding the slot through the swap cooldown. A lane a client used within one idle timeout keeps its normal window. New metric `flexinfer_jobs_lane_releases_total{lane,result}`; the Model CRD gains `status.releasedAt`.
