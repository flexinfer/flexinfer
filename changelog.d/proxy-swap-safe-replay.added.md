- Replay idempotent requests caught in a lane swap in flexinfer-proxy (D6-a). For `/v1/embeddings`, `/v1/rerank`, `/rerank`, `/v1/score` and non-streaming `/v1/audio/transcriptions`, a 502/503/504 that coincides with a swap of the lane (the runtime's drain answer, a parked or not-Ready lane, or a preemption or reload in the last minute) is held back and the request is replayed once: on the `flexinfer.ai/unavailable-reroute-to` equivalent at once when the lane is parked, otherwise on the lane once it serves again, bounded by `FLEXINFER_PROXY_SWAP_REPLAY_WAIT` (30 s), the lane's first-byte budget and the client's deadline. The replay carries `X-FlexInfer-Replayed: <lane>-><served>` and is counted in `flexinfer_proxy_replays_total{lane,outcome}`; `FLEXINFER_PROXY_SWAP_REPLAY=false` turns it off (`internal/proxy/swap_replay.go`, `internal/proxy/routing.go`, `internal/proxy/metrics.go`).
