- Let a shared-GPU member stand in for a down Model on another card. A Model annotated `flexinfer.ai/failover-for: <model>` ranks at the higher of the two `gpu.priority` values in its group's election, and inherits that Model's demand, while that Model is Failed or its pinned node is GPU-degraded. A warm copy of the workhorse can then preempt a creative tenant when the always-on card drops; the target's phase changes re-elect the stand-in's group (`controllers/model_shared_gpu.go`, `controllers/model_controller.go`).
