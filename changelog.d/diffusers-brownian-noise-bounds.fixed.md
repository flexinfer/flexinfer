- `server-diffusers.py` keeps diffusers' Brownian-tree noise queries inside the tree's `[sigma_min, sigma_max]` interval. On the runtime image (Python 3.12, torch 2.13, torchsde 0.2.6), Stable Audio Open's final step to sigma 0 queried below the interval, and torchsde recursed until `RecursionError`, so the lane crashed in-pod (S9). Out-of-range endpoints are clamped. An empty clamped interval returns zeros, which is exact because that final update multiplies the noise by 0. Queries inside the interval are unchanged.
