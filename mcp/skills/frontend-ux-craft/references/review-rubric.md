# Frontend UX Review Rubric

Score or discuss the surface across these dimensions:

## Clarity

- Is the primary action obvious?
- Can a new user explain the screen in one sentence?

## Hierarchy

- Is there a clear reading order?
- Are size, contrast, spacing, and placement doing useful work?

## Interaction

- Do controls look interactive?
- Are transitions and feedback timely and meaningful?

## State Coverage

- Are loading, empty, error, success, hover, focus, active, and disabled states handled?

## Accessibility

- Are semantics, keyboard paths, contrast, and focus indicators present?

## Responsiveness

- Does the layout still feel deliberate on narrow screens?

## Taste

- Does the visual direction feel specific rather than interchangeable?
