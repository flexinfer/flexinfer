# Facilitation Notes

## Diverge First

Do not jump straight to the average answer. Let each lens produce strong opinions before converging.

## Then Converge

Look for recommendations that satisfy multiple lenses at once. Favor moves that improve clarity, interaction quality, and implementation leverage together.

## Recommended Output Shape

- Artifact under review
- User outcome
- Lens-by-lens findings
- Top recommendations
- Chosen next slice
- Evidence / proof
- Open risks
