## The discriminating items (disjoint error sets, again)

- **wp-7** (overtime pay, multi-step): fast **✗** (answered wrong), reasoning **✓**.
  The reasoning lane earns its keep on multi-step arithmetic.
- **cnt-1** (count the digit 1 from 1–50): fast **✓**, reasoning **✗** — qwen35 again
  tried to *enumerate* (40.9 s, the same failure mode as the hard tier's `hcount-1`).
- **cmb-1** (distinct arrangements of LEVEL): fast **✓** (30), reasoning **✗** (answered
  60 — missed the repeated letters).

Every other item: both lanes correct. The traps the router routes to the fast
lane (sheep "all but 9", apples "take away", cats/mice rate) were caught by
**both** lanes here, so they cost nothing either way.

## Takeaway

Same fault line as the tuning set, on data neither lane nor the router had seen:
the fast lane is excellent and fastest; the reasoning lane wins multi-step word
problems but loses counting/enumeration and (here) a repeated-letter
combinatorics item. Disjoint errors → an oracle scores 24/24, and the held-out
routing analysis shows the frozen router recovers it.
